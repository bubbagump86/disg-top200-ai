### The judgement calls that change the answer

- **Preferred Networks** — valuation adjusted from $1.1B to —. The US$1.08B mark came from a Japanese VC landscape report, not a company-disclosed round post-money, and the dedicated DISG-40 refresh found no gradeable published figure (Japan Times reports a 'billion-dollar valuation' without a number). Private valuations here must be the post-money of the latest confirmed round, so the figure is not used.
- **OpenEvidence** — valuation adjusted from $15.0B to $12.0B. A confirmed US$12B Series D post-money from the company announcement (Jan 2026) is the latest confirmed round. A later US$15B round was press-reported only, with no company release, so it is not a confirmed mark; it stays in the notes.
- **Liquid AI** — valuation adjusted from $2.4B to $2.0B. The US$2.3B figure came from data providers, not from the round. Bloomberg reported the December 2024 Series A at 'more than US$2 billion', so the gradeable figure is the US$2.0B floor and the valuation is a reported one, not a confirmed post-money. The round is also more than 18 months old, so it is stale on the revenue audit's age check.
- **Safe Superintelligence** — valuation adjusted from $32.0B to $32.0B. The US$32B post-money was reported by Bloomberg and Reuters for the NVIDIA-led strategic investment, and by TechCrunch for the April 2025 round; the parties did not disclose it. The number stands, but it is a reported valuation, not a company-confirmed one.
- **Dify** — valuation adjusted from $0.2B to —. The company announced a US$30M Series Pre-A on 10 Mar 2026 but disclosed no valuation. The ~US$180M figure is a third-party estimate (Caplight/ToolJunction) derived from funding-round data, and the brief does not accept tracker-derived valuations. No published post-money exists, so no valuation is recorded.
- **LlamaIndex** — valuation adjusted from $0.1B to —. The US$19M Series A (4 Mar 2025, Norwest-led) is a confirmed round, but LlamaIndex has never published a post-money. The ~US$93M figure carried by investor databases (CB Insights/PitchBook) is a third-party estimate and is not used.
- **Sesame AI** — valuation adjusted from $1.2B to $1.0B. Bloomberg reported in Apr 2025 that Sequoia and Spark were in talks to invest ~US$200M 'at a valuation above US$1 billion'. The US$1.19bn post-money carried by investor databases is a computed figure, not a published one, so the US$1.0B floor is used instead.
- **DataRobot** — valuation adjusted from $1.1B to $1.1B. The US$1.1B post-money comes from a September 2026 secondary tender at US$4.2073 per share, priced from Forge Global private-company data. It is a real transaction rather than a company-announced round, so it is flagged reported; it is also a steep down round from the US$6.3B Series G post-money of Jul 2021.
- **Physical Intelligence** — valuation adjusted from $11.0B to $11.5B. The US$20B figure is a round in talks, not a completed round (Bloomberg, 27 Oct 2026: at least US$1B at roughly US$20B, Avenir and Lux in talks to lead, not finalised). The last completed round was struck at a US$11.5B valuation earlier in 2026, so that is the figure used, with the talks recorded here.
- **Abridge** — valuation adjusted from $5.3B to $5.3B. The US$5.3B mark is press-reported (Fierce Healthcare on the Series E, Fortune on the April 2026 extension), not a company-disclosed post-money, so it is a reported valuation. It is also more than 18 months old and is flagged for re-search on the age check.
- **Together AI** — valuation adjusted from $1,150B to $100.0B. The US$1.15B figure is annual BOOKINGS — signed contracts for future services, disclosed with the July 2026 Series C — and bookings are not revenue. The company's own last disclosed ARR was US$100M at the February 2025 Series B, so that is the figure used here; it is 21 months old and is flagged on the revenue audit's age check. The ~US$1B revenue circulating online is a third-party estimate and is not used.
- **Manus AI** — valuation adjusted from $100.0B to $500.0B. The company's own last stated figure was US$100M ARR / US$125M revenue run-rate (company blog, Dec 2025). The higher figure is usable after all: The Information reported an annualised revenue run-rate of about US$500M in June 2026, and Reuters and DealStreet Asia carry the same reporting — it is a named credible outlet, not a tracker. The 2026-06 run-rate is the later figure, so it is used; the company's Dec-2025 numbers are recorded here.

1. **SpaceX is kept as a scored company.** It now owns SpaceXAI and Cursor, so it is AI-software-bearing, but it would normally be excluded as space and defence hardware. It is not a guaranteed place — it competes on composite. xAI and Cursor/Anysphere also appear as separate rows because they are named DISG Top 40 accounts with a guaranteed place, even though the exclusion rule would otherwise fold them into SpaceX.
2. **Palantir is excluded under R4.** Its AIP product is an add-on to a long-established data platform, and the brief's kept-despite-the-rules list names the DISG enterprise-platform accounts (Databricks, ServiceNow, Snowflake, Datadog, Workato, AvePoint) but not Palantir. It would otherwise rank inside the Top 10, so this is the single most consequential exclusion in the refresh. If DISG judges AIP to be a core product rather than an add-on, say so and it comes straight back in.
3. **Google DeepMind and Microsoft Research Asia are shown unscored.** No standalone figures exist, so they cannot carry a composite. They hold guaranteed places as DISG accounts and are displayed directly below Alphabet and Microsoft respectively; that placement is a display convention, not a score.
4. **SpaceX's figure is a whole-company number.** Its revenue and valuation include the space business, which the AI team does not cover. It is kept for completeness but its composite is not an AI figure.
5. **Duolingo is excluded under R4** as an established education app that added an AI product line. It is a borderline case and is logged as such.
